Advertising & ads disclosure

Thuto Notes is free, and advertising is what keeps it that way. This page explains who serves the ads, what is collected, and how you can control it.

Last updated: 15 July 2026
In short: ads are served by Google AdMob so the app can stay free for every learner. Thuto Notes never sees your raw advertising data, ads appear only at natural breaks, and you can switch off personalised ads on your device using the steps below.

1Why you see ads

Thuto Notes is free to download and free to use, with no paywall on any core learning feature. Running AI note generation and homework help costs money, so the app is funded by advertising. Showing ads is how a learner who cannot pay anything can still use the whole app — and how the app pays for itself.

Ads are placed only at genuine breaks in your studying — for example after a note is saved, after a quiz is completed, or when you leave an exam — never in the middle of a question or a worked solution.

2Ad provider — Google AdMob

Advertisements in Thuto Notes are provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google AdMob may collect the following from your device:

  • Your device’s advertising ID (GAID on Android / IDFA on iOS)
  • Approximate location (country / region)
  • Device type, operating-system version, and language
  • Ad interaction data (ad views and clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google LLC under its own privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy. Thuto Notes does not have access to any raw advertising data collected by Google.

3Rewarded ads & free limits

To keep AI features free while covering their cost, the app includes a small daily allowance of AI homework answers. When you reach the daily limit, you can choose to watch an optional rewarded advertisement to unlock additional answers. Watching a rewarded ad is always your choice — you are never forced to view one to keep studying with your saved content.

4How to opt out of personalised ads

You can turn off personalised advertising at any time from your device settings. You will still see ads, but they will not be personalised based on your advertising ID.

Android

Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation (or reset your advertising ID).

iOS

Settings → Privacy → Apple Advertising → turn off Personalised Ads.

5Crash reporting — Firebase Crashlytics

To help fix bugs and improve the app, Thuto Notes uses Firebase Crashlytics to record app crashes. When the app crashes, the following is sent to Firebase:

  • What went wrong (the error message)
  • Which screen or component crashed
  • An anonymous ID (not linked to your name or email)

This data is anonymous and is used only to fix bugs. It is never shared publicly and is never used for advertising.

6app-ads.txt & authorised sellers

To fight ad fraud and protect the app’s advertising, Thuto Notes publishes an app-ads.txt file on its developer website, following the IAB Tech Lab standard. It declares the authorised seller of the app’s ad inventory:

google.com, pub-4652448151681646, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0

This confirms that Google AdMob (publisher ID pub-4652448151681646) is authorised to sell ads for Thuto Notes.

7Children & advertising

Thuto Notes is designed for educational use by students, who may include users under the age of 13. Where our audience may include children, we work to ensure advertising is handled in an age-appropriate, non-personalised manner in line with Google’s policies and applicable law. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about the ads a child is seeing, please contact us.
