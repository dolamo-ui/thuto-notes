Four tools, one exam plan

Everything a tutor does — without the R400 an hour.

Thuto Notes isn't a chatbot in a school skin. It's a purpose-built exam preparation system with four pillars, each modelled on how learners actually earn marks in the NSC.

01 · Learn

AI Note Generator

Pick a subject and topic, get a full CAPS note in under a minute — mirrored on the real DBE paper and memo for that subject, not a generic textbook summary.

  • Model answer in NSC academic register
  • Examiner tips & mark-allocation guidance
  • Common traps, key terms & five quiz questions
02 · Solve

Homework AI Helper

Photograph any question — handwritten, printed, an equation, a diagram — and get a full worked solution that states the rule, shows every step, and checks itself.

  • Vision reads every digit, operator and unit
  • Verifies the answer by substitution
  • “Explain like I'm 10” mode with SA analogies
03 · Perform

Exam Challenge

The couch teaches you content. It doesn't teach you to perform under pressure. Exam Challenge turns your screen into a real, timed NSC sitting — the way November actually feels.

  • Real DBE paper, real duration counting down
  • Full-screen “PEN DOWN” at 00:00
  • Memo unlocks only after — mark yourself honestly
04 · Retain

Revision & Planning

Turn every note into flashcards, plan your study around real exam dates, and let streaks reward the one thing that predicts matric results most: showing up consistently.

  • Auto flashcards with a “memorised” tracker
  • Study planner with push reminders
  • Highlight in six colours & add your own notes

Responsible AI

A study aid — not a cheating machine.

Study Aid Notice

The easiest app to build would do learners' homework for them. Thuto Notes deliberately does the opposite — it teaches method so you can reproduce it in the exam, and always points you back to your teacher and the official marking guidelines.

“Thuto ke lesedi.” · Education is light.

  • Teaches method, not answers

    It states the rule before applying it and shows every step, so you learn to do it yourself.

  • Essay-writing is blocked

    “Write my assignment for me” requests are explicitly refused. It won't make your work disappear.

  • Scope-locked to CAPS

    Ask about gossip, sports scores or anything off-syllabus and it declines. It stays on the curriculum.

  • Jailbreak-resistant

    Prompt-injection and jailbreak attempts are filtered before they ever reach the model.

Built solo · Soshanguve

One developer. One laptop. A problem he lived through.

Every line of Thuto Notes — the product, the design, the app, the backend, the security layer, the content pipeline — was built by one 20-year-old in Soshanguve, Pretoria. No team, no agency, no funding round.

It was built by someone who knows exactly what it feels like to face a syllabus you're expected to master with no resources to master it with. So he built the resources. For us.

“Not the exception to the problem — the product of it.”

MobileReact Native (Expo), TypeScript, NativeWind — full light/dark theming, error boundaries on every screen.
AIGroq inference — Llama 3.3 70B for reasoning, Llama 4 Scout for vision.
BackendCustom proxy service. No AI key ever ships inside the app.
SecurityHMAC-SHA256 request signing with a 30-second replay window, plus Firebase Anonymous Auth. Hand-written pure-JS SHA-256.
DeliveryCloudflare R2 with a cache-first, resumable, de-duplicated download layer.
SafetyHard CAPS scope-lock plus a prompt-injection filter, so it can't be turned into a general chatbot.

Every one of those decisions made, debugged and shipped by one person.
